## Remaining Limitations

- The head model needs validation on **real, approved Indian Railway CCTV** footage.
- The public training dataset (**RPEE-Heads**) may not match Indian platform footage
  (camera angle, lighting, crowd density, resolution) — domain shift is expected.
- Low-resolution and heavily occluded passengers can still be missed.
- Tracking small head boxes causes more ID switches than full-body tracking.
- Zone/line thresholds and geometry need **station-specific tuning**, especially for head
  mode where boxes are much smaller.
- The reported mAP/precision/recall are **RPEE-Heads validation metrics**, not accuracy on
  this sample video or on Indian Railway CCTV. No production accuracy is claimed.
- This is an **MVP / pilot prototype, not a production system.** No live RTSP CCTV
  integration, and all legal/privacy approvals must be obtained before real CCTV use.

## Demo Readiness Verdict

**Mostly demo-ready, with clearly stated limitations.**

Everything required for the demo runs end to end on the real sample video: both detection
modes produce annotated videos and analytics databases, the comparison script generates
side-by-side outputs, reports export with `detector_mode`, the API responds, and the
Streamlit app boots and presents an honest, non-technical body-vs-head comparison. The
Colab-trained `best.pt` is verified (hash-matched, single-class head detector) and is the
weights file used by head mode.

It is **not** "production-ready," and the demo should be presented as a working prototype:
the head model's accuracy has only been measured on a public dataset's validation split, not
on Indian Railway CCTV, and body-vs-head counts are not directly comparable until the line/
zone geometry is calibrated per mode. With those caveats stated up front, the system is
suitable to show and send to the boss.
